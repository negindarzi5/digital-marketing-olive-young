Digital Marketing and Consumer Purchase Intention: A Case Study of CJ Olive Young

Author: Negin Darzi
B.A. Business Administration, Shomal University, Iran
Independent Undergraduate Research Project · 2026

---

About the Research

This qualitative and exploratory research examines the role of digital marketing strategies in influencing consumer purchase intention, focusing on CJ Olive Young as a case study.

The research explores how social media marketing, consumer engagement, brand awareness, and omnichannel strategies can contribute to consumer purchase intention and brand performance.

Methodology

- Literature Review
- Documentary Analysis
- Case Study Analysis
- Secondary Data Analysis

No primary surveys, interviews, questionnaires, experiments, or direct observations were conducted.

Research Focus

Digital Marketing · Social Media Marketing · Consumer Behavior · Purchase Intention · Brand Awareness · Consumer Engagement · Omnichannel Marketing

Research Question

«How do digital marketing strategies influence consumer purchase intention in the case of CJ Olive Young?»
